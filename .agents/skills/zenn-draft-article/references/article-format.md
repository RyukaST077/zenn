# Zenn article format

Use YAML front matter with `title`, `emoji`, `type: tech`, `topics`, and `published: false`. Organize the body around the target reader's concrete problem and the outcome supported by the evidence. Explain prerequisites and new concepts, the minimum reproducible steps, observed results and their meaning, relevant failures and fixes, limitations, and references. Choose headings for the reader's goal rather than the execution log's order.

Label file paths, working directories, and fenced code languages. Keep each command near its recorded output and interpretation. Include necessary versions, setup, authentication, cost, and material limitations where they affect the step or conclusion. Move audit-only logs and secondary comparisons to supporting details. Cite URLs close to external claims. Never invent first-person experience, untested simplified commands, or missing evidence. Never include `<!-- 要素材 -->` in a completed draft.
