# Agent know-how research contract

Include:

- research date and explicit constraints
- existing repository article exclusions
- searched queries and representative strong existing coverage
- official or primary sources, with community sources used only as hypotheses
- candidate claims, exclusions, and reasons
- selected falsifiable claim and target provider
- one concrete reader and the situation in which the uncertainty matters
- the reader's current problem and the decision or action the article promises to enable
- what official documentation or existing articles already answer, and the precise remaining coverage gap
- likely article type: `how-to`, `practice-validation`, `failure`, `new-feature`, `configuration-harness`, or `comparison`
- local feasibility, proposed mode, expected evidence, and practical mapping from any fixture or marker to real work
- safety, cost, and stop conditions

Record a URL and access date for every current external fact. Reject the topic when no honest coverage gap or practical reader value remains after research.
